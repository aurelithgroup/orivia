For newcomers to Dubai and Edinburgh

Your first weeks in a new country, one step at a time.

Orivia guides newcomers through real tasks, like getting a residence ID, registering with a doctor or opening a bank account, and helps the moment something goes wrong. No app to download. English and Arabic, with French, Filipino, Hindi and Urdu in beta.

How it works
  1. Scan where you arrive. A QR code at the airport, on campus or in an arrival email opens Orivia, already set to your city.
  2. Orivia works out where you are. One question, “What’s the last thing you remember doing?”, places you on the process. You don’t need to know the official names.
  3. Step by step, with help at every step. Each step says what to do and what to bring. If something goes wrong, “I need help” shows what to do next and who to ask.
What makes it different

Help when it goes wrong

Most guides stop at the instructions. Orivia covers the missed appointment, the gate that won’t open and the clinic that says you’re not covered.

It already knows the step you’re on

Search engines and chatbots need you to describe the problem, in your second language, under stress. Orivia needs a tap.

Made for the real-world moment

Cards to show staff in the local language, tap-to-call numbers, and it keeps working without internet.

For universities and organisations

Orivia handles the routine questions. Your team handles the people.

Put an Orivia QR code in your arrival email or at your desk. Newcomers get step-by-step help for the common tasks, and are sent to your team when a person is needed.

Orivia is guidance, not official or legal advice. Always check the official source.

Privacy: Orivia sends nothing anywhere. Progress and answers stay on your own phone.
