How it works
  1. Tell Orivia what’s happening. Ask in your own words. Add your location and background when they help.
  2. Let her do the research. Orivia looks for relevant information and shows which sources support the answer.
  3. Take your next step. Save a reminder, explore a guide, prepare a message or open a destination in maps.
What makes it different

Help when it goes wrong

Most guides stop at the instructions. Orivia covers the missed appointment, the gate that won’t open and the clinic that says you’re not covered.

Built around your situation

Continue the conversation, correct a detail or ask what comes next. You don’t need to know the official terms.

Made for the real-world moment

Cards to show staff in the local language, tap-to-call numbers, and links that open your maps app. Chat and research need internet.

It remembers for you

A home screen that shows where you are, what you’re waiting on and what’s next. Keep the reminders you choose in your Orivia calendar.

Take me there

Checked addresses, hours and phone numbers for the places each step sends you, opening straight into your maps app.

Built for institutions

We’re developing institution-specific experiences, dashboards and integrations. They are planned features, not live services yet.

Trust you can see

Research answers link to the sources Orivia opened and explain what remains uncertain.

For universities and organisations

Orivia handles the routine questions. Your team handles the people.

Put an Orivia QR code in your arrival email or at your desk. Newcomers get step-by-step help for the common tasks, and are sent to your team when a person is needed.

Fewer repeat questions

The “what do I do next?” questions that fill inboxes every September get answered on the spot, in the newcomer’s language.

Safe by design

No account needed. Chats stay on the newcomer’s phone, messages go to our AI providers only with their agreement, and research answers link to the sources used.

Your instructions, on top

Planned: institution-provided instructions, contacts and deadlines alongside Orivia’s general guidance.

See where people get stuck

Planned institution dashboards could show aggregate patterns without exposing personal chats. These dashboards are not live yet.
