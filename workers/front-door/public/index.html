Before you begin

A few good questions.

Do I need to download an app?

You can open Orivia in your browser. A native mobile app is part of the longer-term plan.

Can I rely on every answer?

Orivia can make mistakes. Open the sources, check dates and confirm important decisions with the relevant authority or organisation. She provides guidance, not official or legal advice.

Who can see my conversations?

The app explains its current storage and AI-processing choices before you start. Organisation membership does not give staff access to your personal chats. Read the in-app privacy information for the version you use.
